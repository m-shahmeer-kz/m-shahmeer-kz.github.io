How I work

From brief to working product,
in four clear steps.

01
Understand

I start with your users and your goal.

Questions first, pixels later. We agree on who it's for, what success looks like and what data we have to work with.

02
Design

Wireframes, then polished screens in Figma.

Flows and layouts you can click through early, so feedback is quick and nothing is a surprise.

03
Build

The intelligent part, built properly.

Models, pipelines and APIs in Python and FastAPI, connected to the interface we designed together.
